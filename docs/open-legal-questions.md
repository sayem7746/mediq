# Open legal questions

**Status:** Launch blockers until counsel answers  
**Owner:** Legal coordinator — MediQ  
**Created:** 2026-09-20  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-20

These questions are for qualified Malaysian and Bangladeshi counsel / privacy advisors. Engineering must not invent answers. Required changes are listed in [legal-required-changes.md](./legal-required-changes.md).

## Entity and operations

1. Which legal entity offers MediQ to Bangladeshi users, and which entity contracts with Malaysian providers?
2. Is a local registration, representative, or licence required in Bangladesh or Malaysia for a non-clinical navigation directory?
3. What advertising and consumer-protection rules apply to hospital listings and sponsored placements?

## Data protection

4. What lawful basis and consent standard apply to enquiry data collected in Bangladesh and stored or accessed from Malaysia or another host region?
5. Are cross-border transfer tools (contractual clauses, local storage, or another mechanism) required?
6. What minimum and maximum retention periods apply to navigation requests, consent events, and audit logs?
7. What deletion / access / correction rights must the product honour, and in what timeframe?

## Consent and copy

8. Are the English and Bangla strings in `content/disclosures.*.json` and `content/fee-disclosure.*.json` adequate?
9. Does the emergency redirect wording create any duty of care MediQ cannot meet?
10. Must fee and sponsored disclosures appear on every listing card, or is a page-level + request-form disclosure enough?

## Commercial

11. Is the mixed revenue model (patient navigation fee + contracted provider listing/admin fee) lawful if both are disclosed and organic rank is payment-neutral?
12. What must the provider agreement include for data processing, audit, and termination?
13. Who is the invoice entity, and what refund language is required?

## Complaints and incidents

14. What complaint route and regulator notice (if any) must be published?
15. What breach-notification clocks apply in each jurisdiction?
